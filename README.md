# Daily Spendable

How much can I safely spend today? One number, every day, that protects your
bills, essentials, savings, debt payments and cash buffer until payday.

## How it works

- **Checking balance** minus unpaid bills due by payday, essentials, savings,
  debt payments and your cash buffer = **spendable cash**.
- Spendable cash ÷ days left until payday = **daily allowance**.
- Daily allowance minus what you've spent today = **what you can spend today**.
- Logging a spend lowers your balance too, so tomorrow's number already
  reflects today. Marking a bill paid takes it out of both the bill list and
  your balance, so paying a bill never inflates your number.
- Bills due *after* payday are listed but not counted yet.
- "Show my math" on the Today screen shows every figure in the calculation.

## Install on your iPhone

1. Open the GitHub Pages link in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
3. It installs with its own icon and opens like a real app (no browser bar).

## Privacy

No account, no server, nothing sent anywhere. All numbers live in your
browser's local storage on your device. Use "Clear everything" in Plan to
wipe it.

## Note on the previous version

An earlier 600KB React build of this app stored data under a different
format and origin, so it does not carry over automatically. The new version
starts fresh with a one-minute setup; "Try sample numbers" shows how it
works before you enter your own figures.
